### About

I study Nuclear Energy Engineering at Hacettepe University, and I write code. I'm still early in
both, but the direction is clear to me: I want to work where reactor physics meets computation —
simulation, scientific computing, and tools that make abstract nuclear concepts visible.

- 🎓 Nuclear Energy Engineering, Hacettepe University (Ankara) — Class of 2029
- 💻 Mobile development background; currently moving toward scientific computing in Python
- 🤝 Former Co-Chair of Corporate Relations, Hacettepe Energy Society
- 🎯 Long-term interest: reactor physics, small modular reactors, and research-oriented work abroad

### What I'm building

**FocusBus** — a focus app that turns a study session into a real intercity bus journey across
Turkey. You pick a route, start studying, and the bus moves along the actual highway in real time.
Built with Flutter. Currently in testing on Google Play; source and store link will land here once
it's out of testing.
